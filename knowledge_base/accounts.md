# Savings and Current Accounts

## Savings account variants and minimum balance
Anil Bank offers three savings variants. Regular Savings needs a monthly average balance (MAB) of Rs 10,000 in metro branches and Rs 5,000 elsewhere. Salary accounts have no minimum balance while salary is credited at least once in 3 months. Anil Prime needs an MAB of Rs 1,00,000 and includes a free locker for the first year.

## Non-maintenance charges
If the MAB is not maintained, a charge of 6% of the shortfall is levied, capped at Rs 500 per month plus GST. No charge is applied in the first two months after account opening.

## Savings interest
Savings balances earn 3.00% a year up to Rs 5 lakh and 3.50% on the portion above Rs 5 lakh. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly.

## Transfers: UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS
UPI transfers are free with a limit of Rs 1 lakh per day. IMPS allows up to Rs 5 lakh per transaction, with a fee of Rs 5 to Rs 15 plus GST. NEFT and RTGS done through net banking or the mobile app are free. RTGS needs a minimum of Rs 2 lakh.

## ATM and debit card
Five free transactions a month at Anil ATMs and three at other banks' ATMs in metro cities. After that, Rs 21 plus GST per transaction. The daily ATM withdrawal limit is Rs 50,000 on the standard debit card and Rs 1,00,000 on the Prime card.

## Account closure
Closing an account within 14 days of opening is free. Between 15 days and 12 months a Rs 500 closure fee applies. After 12 months closure is free.
