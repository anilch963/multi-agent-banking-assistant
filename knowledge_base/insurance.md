# Insurance

## Term life: Anil Secure Term
Pure protection cover from Rs 50 lakh to Rs 5 crore for ages 18 to 65, with policy terms up to age 85. Optional riders: critical illness, accidental death and waiver of premium. Premiums qualify for Section 80C deduction in the old tax regime. Suicide is excluded in the first 12 months.

## Health: Anil Health Shield
Individual and family floater plans with cover from Rs 3 lakh to Rs 1 crore. Cashless treatment at over 9,000 network hospitals. Waiting periods: 30 days for illnesses (accidents are covered from day one), 24 months for specified procedures and 36 months for pre-existing diseases. A 10% no-claim bonus is added each claim-free year up to 50%. Premiums qualify for Section 80D deduction.

## Motor: Anil Drive Protect
Comprehensive cover for own damage and third-party liability. Add-ons: zero depreciation, engine protect, roadside assistance. No-claim bonus ranges from 20% after one claim-free year to 50% after five. Third-party cover is mandatory by law.

## Premium payment, grace period and lapse
Yearly premium policies have a 30-day grace period after the due date; monthly policies have 15 days. Cover continues during the grace period. If the premium is still unpaid the policy lapses. A lapsed life policy can be revived within 5 years by paying overdue premiums with interest; a lapsed health policy loses its waiting-period credit and no-claim bonus.

## How to make a claim
Cashless health claims: show the e-card at a network hospital; planned admissions need pre-authorisation 48 hours in advance and emergencies within 24 hours of admission. Reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 days of discharge with bills and the discharge summary. Life claims need the death certificate, policy document and nominee's identity proof; most are settled within 30 days. Motor claims must be reported within 48 hours, before repairs start.

## Free-look period and nominee changes
Every new policy has a 30-day free-look period to cancel for a refund less stamp duty and risk premium for days covered. The nominee can be changed any time in the app or at a branch at no cost.

## Insurance is not an investment recommendation
Anil Bank is a corporate agent for insurers. Buying insurance is voluntary and is not linked to any other banking facility.
