# Security and Fraud

## The bank will never ask for these
Anil Bank never asks for your OTP, PIN, CVV, full card number or net-banking password by phone, SMS, email or chat, and the assistant will never ask for them either. Do not share them with anyone, including people claiming to be bank staff.

## Reporting an unauthorised transaction
Report it immediately through the assistant, the app or the 24x7 helpline 1800-000-0000. Block the affected card or channel first, then raise a dispute. A dispute needs the transaction date, amount and merchant.

## Customer liability for unauthorised transactions
If the fraud is reported within 3 working days, the customer has zero liability and the amount is credited back provisionally within 10 working days. Reported in 4 to 7 working days, liability is limited to Rs 10,000 for savings accounts and cards with limits up to Rs 5 lakh, and Rs 25,000 otherwise. After 7 working days liability is decided under the bank's board-approved policy. If the loss is due to the customer sharing credentials, the customer bears the loss until it is reported.

## Chargebacks and disputes
Card disputes for goods not received, duplicate charges or wrong amounts must be raised within 60 days of the statement date. Resolution takes up to 45 days for domestic and 90 days for international transactions.

## Staying safe
Use the official app only, enable transaction alerts, set card limits and switch off international and online usage when not needed. Be cautious of screen-sharing apps, QR codes sent to "receive" money and links offering KYC updates.
