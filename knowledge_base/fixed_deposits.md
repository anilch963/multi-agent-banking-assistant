# Fixed Deposits

## Interest rates by tenure
Current FD rates per annum: 1 to 5 months 6.00%; 6 to 11 months 6.50%; 12 to 23 months 6.75%; 24 to 35 months 7.10%; 36 to 120 months 7.25%. Senior citizens (60 years and above) earn an extra 0.50% on every slab. Interest is compounded quarterly on cumulative deposits.

## Minimum amount and tenure
The minimum deposit is Rs 5,000 and the tenure can be from 1 month to 10 years. Deposits can be opened from a savings or current account in the app. Auto-renewal can be set at booking.

## Premature withdrawal
An FD can be closed early. The interest paid is the rate applicable for the period the deposit actually ran, minus a 1.00% penalty. No interest is paid if the deposit is closed within 7 days. Partial withdrawal is allowed in multiples of Rs 1,000.

## Tax on FD interest (TDS)
TDS at 10% is deducted when total interest across deposits exceeds Rs 50,000 in a financial year (Rs 1,00,000 for senior citizens). Without a PAN the rate is 20%. Eligible customers can submit Form 15G or 15H at the start of the year to avoid TDS.

## Tax-saver FD
The tax-saver FD has a 5-year lock-in, a maximum of Rs 1.5 lakh a year and qualifies for deduction under Section 80C in the old tax regime. It cannot be closed early or used as loan collateral.

## Loan or overdraft against FD
Up to 90% of the deposit value can be borrowed at 1.00% above the FD rate, with no processing fee. The deposit keeps earning interest.
