# Recurring Deposits

## How an RD works
A fixed instalment is debited from your account every month for the chosen tenure. The minimum instalment is Rs 500, in multiples of Rs 100, for 6 to 120 months. RD rates are the same as FD rates for the same tenure, with 0.50% extra for senior citizens, and interest is compounded quarterly.

## Missed instalments
A missed instalment attracts a penalty of Rs 1.50 per Rs 100 per month of delay. If six consecutive instalments are missed the RD is closed and the balance is paid to the linked account at the premature-withdrawal rate.

## Premature closure
An RD can be closed early with a 1.00% penalty on the applicable rate. Partial withdrawal is not allowed. Closure within 3 months earns only the savings account rate.

## RD or SIP-style goal planning
RDs suit short-term goals of 1 to 3 years where capital safety matters, such as a vacation, a down payment or an emergency fund top-up. Returns are fixed and not market linked. Interest is taxable at the customer's slab rate and TDS rules are the same as for FDs.

## Loan against RD
Up to 85% of the balance accumulated in the RD can be borrowed at 1.50% above the RD rate.
