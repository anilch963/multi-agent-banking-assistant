# Credit Cards

## Card range and annual fees
Anil Starter Cashback: no joining fee, annual fee Rs 499 waived on yearly spends of Rs 50,000; 1% cashback on all spends. Anil Platinum Rewards: annual fee Rs 1,499 waived on spends of Rs 2 lakh; 4 reward points per Rs 150. Anil Travel Elite: annual fee Rs 4,999 waived on spends of Rs 8 lakh; 8 domestic and 4 international lounge visits a year and a 2% forex markup. Anil Business Signature: annual fee Rs 2,999; 5 points per Rs 150 on business categories.

## Interest-free period and finance charges
The interest-free period is 20 to 50 days and applies only if the previous bill was paid in full. Unpaid balances attract finance charges of 3.5% a month (42% a year) from the transaction date. Paying only the minimum due avoids late fees but interest continues on the remaining balance.

## Minimum due and late payment fees
Minimum amount due is 5% of the outstanding balance or Rs 200, whichever is higher. Late payment fees: nil for dues up to Rs 500, Rs 500 for Rs 501 to Rs 5,000, Rs 750 for Rs 5,001 to Rs 25,000 and Rs 1,200 above Rs 25,000.

## Reward points
One reward point is worth Rs 0.25 when redeemed for statement credit and up to Rs 0.50 for flights and hotels on the Anil travel portal. Points expire 36 months after they are earned. Fuel, rent and wallet loads do not earn points.

## Converting purchases to EMI
Purchases of Rs 2,500 or more can be converted to EMI within 30 days for 3, 6, 9, 12, 18 or 24 months at 15% a year, with a one-time processing fee of 1% (minimum Rs 199).

## Cash withdrawal and forex
Cash advances cost 2.5% of the amount (minimum Rs 500) and attract interest from day one. Foreign-currency transactions carry a 3.5% markup, except Travel Elite at 2%.

## Lost or stolen card
Block the card immediately in the app or through the assistant, or call the 24x7 helpline 1800-000-0000. A replacement card costs Rs 200 and arrives in 5 to 7 working days. Transactions after the block request are not the customer's liability.

## Credit limit increase
A limit increase can be requested after 6 months of on-time payments. It is assessed on income, repayment history and utilisation; keeping utilisation under 30% improves the chances.
