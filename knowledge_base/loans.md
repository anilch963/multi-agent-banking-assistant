# Loans

## Personal loan
Up to Rs 25 lakh for 12 to 60 months, from 13.50% a year. Minimum credit score 700 and verified KYC. Processing fee 2% of the loan amount. Salaried applicants need a net monthly income of at least Rs 25,000. Disbursal is usually within 2 working days of approval.

## Home loan
Up to Rs 5 crore for up to 30 years, from 8.60% a year (floating, linked to the repo rate). Funding is up to 80% of the property value (90% for loans under Rs 30 lakh). Processing fee 0.50%, maximum Rs 15,000. Minimum credit score 700.

## Car loan
Up to Rs 50 lakh for up to 7 years, from 9.25% a year. Up to 90% of the on-road price for new cars. Minimum credit score 680. Processing fee Rs 3,500.

## Education loan
Up to Rs 75 lakh for up to 15 years, from 10.50% a year. Repayment starts 12 months after the course ends or 6 months after getting a job, whichever is earlier. Loans above Rs 7.5 lakh need collateral. Minimum co-applicant credit score 650.

## How eligibility is assessed
Total EMIs, including the new loan, should not exceed 50% of net monthly income (the fixed-obligation-to-income ratio, FOIR). Credit score, KYC status, employment stability and existing obligations are also considered. Any eligibility figure shown by the assistant is indicative; final sanction depends on underwriting and documents.

## Prepayment and foreclosure
Floating-rate home loans have no prepayment charges. Personal loans can be foreclosed after 12 EMIs with a 3% fee on the outstanding principal. Car loans carry a 4% foreclosure fee in the first two years and 2% afterwards. Part-prepayments reduce either the tenure or the EMI, at the customer's choice.

## Missed EMI
A missed EMI attracts penal charges of 2% a month on the overdue amount plus a Rs 500 bounce charge, and is reported to credit bureaus after 30 days. Customers in difficulty should contact the bank early to discuss restructuring.

## Documents required
Identity and address proof, PAN, last 3 months' salary slips and 6 months' bank statements for salaried applicants; 2 years' ITR and business proof for self-employed applicants; property papers for home loans.
