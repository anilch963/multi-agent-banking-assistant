# KYC and Grievance Redressal

## KYC documents and re-KYC
KYC needs a PAN and one officially valid address document. Video KYC is available in the app from 9 am to 9 pm. Re-KYC is due every 2 years for high-risk, 8 years for medium-risk and 10 years for low-risk customers. Accounts with pending KYC are limited to a balance of Rs 1 lakh and cannot be used for new loans, cards or deposits above Rs 50,000.

## Raising a complaint
Complaints can be raised through the assistant, the app, branches or the helpline. A ticket number is issued immediately. Standard resolution time is 7 working days; card disputes and fraud cases follow their own timelines.

## Escalation levels
Level 1: customer care. Level 2: the Nodal Officer, if unresolved after 7 working days. Level 3: the Principal Nodal Officer, if unresolved after a further 5 working days. If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days in total, the customer may approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme free of charge.

## Updating contact details and nominee
Mobile number, email and address can be updated in the app with OTP and document verification. Nominee details for accounts and deposits can be added or changed at any time at no cost.

## Deceased customer claims
Claims on accounts with a nominee are settled within 15 days of receiving the death certificate and the nominee's KYC. Without a nominee, a succession certificate or indemnity is required depending on the amount.
